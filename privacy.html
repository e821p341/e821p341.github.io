Privacy Policy

To-Do Widget · Last updated October 28, 2026

To-Do Widget ("the app") is a personal Windows desktop utility that displays your to-do items on the desktop. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and where it is kept. The short version: everything stays on your own computer.

Information the app accesses

The app only reads sources you point it at. Depending on what you connect, that can include:

  • Local files - plain-text to-do lists and Excel spreadsheets in the folder you choose.
  • Google Tasks (Google Tasks API, scope tasks) - your task lists and the tasks in them: titles, notes, due dates, parent/child relationships and completion status. The app updates a task's completion status when you tick or untick it in the widget, and would create or edit a task only when you explicitly do so from the widget.
  • Gmail (Gmail API, scope gmail.readonly) - only messages you have starred, and only their message ID, thread ID, subject line, sender and date, so that each starred message can be shown as a to-do that links back to the conversation. Message bodies and attachments are never requested or downloaded. If you turn on the optional "remove the star when I complete it" setting, the app asks for the gmail.modify scope and uses it for one thing: removing the Starred label from a message you completed in the widget. It makes no other change to your mail.

How the information is used

Solely to display your tasks in the widget on your computer and to write a completion back to the source you completed it from. The app does not use your information for anything else: no advertising, no profiling, no analytics, and no training of any kind.

Where it is stored

  • Task data is cached in a local database (SQLite) and a local JSON snapshot inside the app's own folder on your PC, so the widget can draw instantly and keep working offline.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored in a local secrets folder on your PC and are used only to call Google's APIs on your behalf.
  • Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any third party. The app has no server of its own; its only network traffic is directly between your computer and Google's APIs.

Sharing

Your information is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone. There are no third-party services, SDKs or trackers in the app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

To-Do Widget's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Cached data is refreshed every few minutes and replaced with the current contents of your sources; items that disappear from a source are retained only in the app's local completed log, for the window you configure (14 days by default).
  • To delete everything, delete the app's folder from your PC. To remove the app's Google access, delete the token file in the secrets folder and revoke "To-Do Widget" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
