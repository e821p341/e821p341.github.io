To-Do Widget

A to-do list that lives on the Windows desktop.

To-Do Widget is a small Windows 11 program that draws your open tasks directly on the desktop layer, underneath every other window, where a sticky note would go. It stays put when you press Win+D, it never steals focus, and you can set its size and transparency to taste.

What it does

  • Gathers tasks from the places they already are: plain-text lists, Excel spreadsheets, Google Tasks, and the Gmail messages you have starred.
  • Groups them by category, colors them by urgency, and shows when each was added, when it is due, and how much time is left (or how overdue it is).
  • Supports sub-tasks, priorities and emoji, and a view of everything by day.
  • Ticking a task in the widget completes it where it came from: the line in the text file, the row in the spreadsheet, or the task in Google Tasks. Clicking a task opens the file, list or message it came from.
  • Keeps a log of what you finished and when.

How it uses your Google account

If you connect a Google account, the widget reads your Google Tasks lists so they appear alongside your other tasks, and updates a task's completion status when you tick it in the widget. It also reads the subject, sender and date of messages you have starred in Gmail so each starred message shows up as a to-do; message bodies and attachments are never downloaded. Everything runs on your own computer and is stored only there. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Availability

To-Do Widget is a personal utility, built in Python and Qt, maintained for the developer's own use. It is not sold or distributed commercially and has no servers, accounts or analytics of its own.
